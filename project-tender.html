Overview

A luxury yacht tender designed to complement superyachts in the 40–60m range. The hull combines a deep-V entry with a flat mid-section for a dry, stable ride at speed.

The design brief demanded a tender that could match the visual language of a flagship superyacht while functioning as a working boat — carrying guests, crew, provisions and dive equipment daily. Storage and seating were designed around these competing demands.
