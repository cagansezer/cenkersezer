Overview

A 42-foot bluewater sailing yacht concept focused on long-range passage making. The hull form prioritises stability and efficiency, while the deck layout creates an uncluttered, safe working environment.

The design draws from traditional offshore racing proportions, refined for comfortable cruising. Every deck fitting is recessed or removed where possible — the result is a boat that sails faster than it looks and demands less effort to operate.
