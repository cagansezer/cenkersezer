Overview

An interior concept for a rear-wheel-drive sports car. The driver-focused cabin wraps around the occupant, placing every control intuitively. Materials blend Alcantara, machined aluminium and sustainable cork.

The challenge was creating a space that feels intimate and precise at speed, while remaining liveable on daily commutes. The material palette balances performance with warmth — tactile surfaces where hands rest, hard surfaces where eyes land.
