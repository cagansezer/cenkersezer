Helix EV

Category

Automotive Design

Year

Material

Composite Body, Recycled Aluminium

Dimensions

L 4,320 × W 1,890 × H 1,240 mm

Overview

An electric sports coupe concept exploring the tension between aerodynamic necessity and sculptural form. The body language is taut and restrained, using light and shadow as primary design tools.

Every surface is resolved to eliminate decorative excess — the form is dictated by airflow, occupant ergonomics, and structural logic. The result is a car that feels inevitable rather than styled.
